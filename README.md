One original single-line joke excusing a missed deadline for the One Line, No
Deadline Met bounty: one line, no setup, no explanation — just the excuse.

> I didn't miss the deadline — I outsourced it to my future self, and he's even lazier than I am.

## Why this line

- **Excuse first.** It opens by denying the miss and immediately supplies the
  excuse (outsourced to future self), so it reads as an excuse, not an apology.
- **Real turn.** "Outsourcing" sounds responsible and corporate until the punch
  reveals the subcontractor is a lazier version of yourself.
- **Punch at the end.** The sentence builds a setup and detonates on the last
  five words, "even lazier than I am."
- **Sounds cornered.** It reads like the exact thing someone says with a shrug
  when asked where the report is — self-deprecating, a little desperate,
  exaggerated just enough.
- **Fresh angle.** No dog ate the homework, no traffic, no Wi-Fi — the joke is
  procrastination rebranded as delegation.
- **Economy.** 95 characters, every word earning its place, nothing left to trim.

See [CANDIDATES.md](CANDIDATES.md) for the 10 candidates and the full reasoning.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the line (95 characters, no trailing newline) to `<MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR>/entry.txt`
(default `/output/entry.txt`). Standard library only, deterministic, no network.
The program re-reads the file and asserts it is a single line of 1–200 characters
whose sha256 matches the line it intended to write.
