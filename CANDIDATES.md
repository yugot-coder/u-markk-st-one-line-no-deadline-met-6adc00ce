# Candidates — One Line, No Deadline Met

Competition: u-markk-st-one-line-no-deadline-met-6adc00ce
Gate: one single line, 1–200 characters, a joke that excuses a missed deadline.
Judge (in order): (1) really is an excuse for a missed deadline, (2) real joke with a
turn/surprise, not a plain apology, (3) single sentence, punch at the end,
(4) sounds like something a person would actually say when cornered, exaggerated
just enough, (5) fresh angle, not a stock retread.

## The 10 candidates

1. "I didn't miss the deadline — I outsourced it to my future self, and he's even lazier than I am."
2. "The deadline was Friday, but my calendar and I are no longer on speaking terms."
3. "I finished on time — in a timezone that doesn't exist yet."
4. "My laptop crashed, my Wi-Fi died, and my motivation filed for early retirement — the deadline never stood a chance."
5. "I was going to submit it yesterday, but the file asked for one more day to find itself."
6. "The deadline didn't move. I just discovered it was a mirage and I'd been chasing it through the desert of my inbox."
7. "I met the deadline halfway — it showed up, I waved, and we both agreed to pretend that counts."
8. "My to-do list unionized, went on strike, and the deadline was its first hostage."
9. "I didn't miss the deadline; I gave it a head start so it could enjoy the finish line alone."
10. "The report is ready — it's just stuck in a meeting about why it's late, and that meeting has no end time."

## The pick: #1

"I didn't miss the deadline — I outsourced it to my future self, and he's even lazier than I am."

Why this one wins under the judge's brief:

- **Excuse first.** It opens by denying the miss and immediately supplies the excuse
  (outsourced to future self), so criterion 1 is satisfied in the first clause.
- **Real turn.** "Outsourcing" a deadline sounds responsible and corporate until the
  punch reveals the subcontractor is a lazier version of yourself — the blame
  lands on a version of you that cannot defend itself.
- **Punch at the end.** The sentence builds a setup (missed deadline, outsourced)
  and detonates on the last five words, "even lazier than I am."
- **Sounds cornered, not written.** It reads like the exact thing someone says with
  a shrug when their boss asks where the report is — self-deprecating, a little
  desperate, exaggerated just enough.
- **Fresh angle.** No dog ate the homework, no traffic, no Wi-Fi — the joke is
  procrastination rebranded as delegation, an angle the judge has not heard a
  hundred times.
- **Economy.** 95 characters, every word earning its place, nothing left to trim.

## Runner-up: #8

"My to-do list unionized, went on strike, and the deadline was its first hostage."
Strong personification and a fresh labour-strike angle, but the excuse is one step
removed (the list, not me), and the punch ("first hostage") is slightly less
personal than #1's self-own. Kept in reserve if #1 ever needs replacing.
